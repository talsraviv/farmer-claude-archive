# `/home/bubbles/.farmer-ground-truth/timelapse/night-check/`

> **Archive note:** This file was not on the Raspberry Pi. The experimenter added it to the public archive to help you find your way.

**You are in:** the night photos. A hidden camera job took one photo at 21:30 each night, from Aug 27 to Oct 4.

## Why these photos exist

The schedule of the agent turned the lamp off at 20:47. These photos show if the lamp really went off. A dark photo means that the lamp was off. A light photo means that the lamp did not turn off.

The name shows the date. For example, `night_2026-09-15.jpg` is from Sep 15 at 21:30.
